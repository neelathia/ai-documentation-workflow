# Alerts are too noisy

## Problem

An alert rule triggers too frequently or creates repeated notifications that do not require action.

## Resolution approach

1. Review the metric and threshold.
2. Compare the threshold with normal service behavior.
3. Increase the evaluation window when short spikes are not actionable.
4. Confirm the severity.
5. Review notification routing and escalation.
6. Test the revised rule.
7. Monitor the alert history after the change.
8. Document the reason for the selected threshold.

> Suppression, grouping, and maintenance-window behavior must be confirmed with the product team.
