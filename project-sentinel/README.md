## Documentation model

The content is organized primarily by task, with secondary access by role and product area. Contextual help in the prototype points users to the standalone Help Center.

## PDF delivery

The documentation strategy is the primary PDF candidate for email submission. The task page may be included as an appendix or sent as a separate PDF.

The HTML files include browser-print support and can also be printed to PDF from a browser.

## Validation status

The prototype is illustrative. Assessment-confirmed components and market-informed proposals are labeled separately.
